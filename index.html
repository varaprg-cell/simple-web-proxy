Our Story

Rooted in tradition,
grown with love

Vennela Farming was founded by our family on a beautiful stretch of land in Madhavarampodu, Railway Kodur, with a deep belief that good food starts with good soil. We planted our first mango saplings with care and patience, and our orchard is steadily growing.

We have not yet sold our first harvest — but that day is coming. When it does, every mango that leaves our farm will be naturally ripened, hand-picked at the right moment, and delivered with pride. No shortcuts. No artificial ripening. Just patience and passion.

Register your interest through our contact form and be among the first to receive our mangoes when we open for orders.

Our Mango Varieties

🥭
Alphonso
King of Mangoes

Rich, creamy, and intensely aromatic with a deep saffron-yellow flesh. Our most prized variety, known for its perfect balance of sweetness and tanginess.

Fresh Eating Export Grade Premium
Season
Apr – Jun
🍋
Kesar
Queen of Mangoes

Saffron-hued pulp with a honey-like sweetness and mild tartness. Excellent for juices, pulp, milkshakes, and desserts. Thick flesh, small seed.

Juice & Pulp Desserts Wholesale
Season
May – Jul
🌿
Totapuri
Parrot Beak Mango

Distinctively shaped with a firm, fiber-free flesh and mildly sweet-tangy flavor. Ideal for pickles, chutneys, and raw preparations. High yield variety.

Pickling Chutney Raw Use
Season
May – Aug
🧡
Banganapalli
Safeda / Benishan

Large, golden-yellow mangoes with a smooth, fiberless pulp and a mild, sweet flavor. One of the most popular table mangoes in South India.

Table Mango Family Pack Local Sale
Season
Apr – Jul
🟡
Neelam
Late Season Gem

Small, oval mangoes with a rich, spicy-sweet aroma and deep orange pulp. The last harvest of the season, beloved for its intense fragrance.

Late Season Aromatic Fresh & Dried
Season
Jun – Aug
🌳
Malgova
Big & Bold

One of the largest mango varieties, with creamy yellow flesh that is mildly sweet and full of juice. A favourite for bulk buyers and fruit stalls.

Bulk Sale Large Size Juicy
Season
May – Jul
